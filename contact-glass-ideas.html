The 10 ideas used here

  1. Aurora backdrop: three slow blurred green blobs behind everything, so the glass has colour to refract.
  2. Specular edge: a 1px white highlight on each card border that follows the cursor.
  3. Inner spotlight: a soft light pool inside the card that tracks the pointer on hover.
  4. Glass service chips: frosted pills that fill forest green with a check and a soft halo when selected.
  5. Sliding budget control: one segmented glass track with a spring-eased thumb instead of four separate buttons.
  6. Sheen button: a diagonal light sweep across the send button on hover.
  7. Copy-to-confirm: the Copy email button turns into "Copied" and recolours for two seconds.
  8. Live availability pill: pulsing green dot and a real UTC clock ticking every second.
  9. Magnetic socials: links drift a few pixels toward the cursor and fill green.
  10. Floating glass dock: a dark translucent nav at the bottom that blurs whatever scrolls under it.
